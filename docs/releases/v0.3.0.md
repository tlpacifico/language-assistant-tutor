## Content since 0.2.0

- **Conversation about an article** (new mode, outside the five phases): **Artigos** page; authorization of automatic sending per session (never pre-selected); recording that only ends by your action; tutor that comments on and goes deeper into each answer; final feedback on coherence, cohesion, development and interaction, with citations from your answers; correction of older answers in new branches; local recovery without sends at startup; tutor voice in chunks with «Parar voz» and «Repetir voz». See [setup](../article-conversation/setup.md) and [validation](../article-conversation/validation.md).
- **SQLite schema v5** (`article_*`): automatic migration from versions 1 to 4, without touching the STAR tables.
- **Helper**: opt-in operations `synthesize_stream` and `cancel`; optional `beamSize` in `transcribe` (default unchanged).
- **Diagnostics**: closed operations `article_transcribe`, `article_reply`, `article_first_audio` and `article_feedback` (duration and code only).

## Limitations and pending items

- **The article conversation has no manual acceptance**: it was never used with a real microphone, real Kokoro voice, real protected credential or paid requests; the usefulness of the feedback, the fidelity of the transcription and the perceived wait remain to be evaluated. The Artigos page had no visual validation.
- The **performance measurements** (`scripts/benchmark-audio.ps1`) and the `beam_size=5/1` comparison remain to be done; the default is maintained.
- Known deviations (article limit of 30,000 bytes, no re-recording as a branch, old branches only in the database): see [validation](../article-conversation/validation.md).
- The **installer was not installed** on this machine: installation, upgrade over v0.2.0 and uninstallation remain to be validated.
- Phases 1 to 4 still have no recorded manual acceptance. Phase 5 has not been started.
- Local mode requires Python 3.11, the `audio-worker` helper and the models prepared outside the repository (see [phase 1 setup](../phase-1/setup.md)). The release does not include them.
