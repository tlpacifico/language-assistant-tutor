## Content since 0.1.0

- **Voice models when the interview opens** (`e5ea83e`): the helper accepts the
  `preload` request of protocol v1 and loads Whisper and Kokoro before the first
  recording, eliminating the wait for the first «Ouvir pergunta» and for the
  first transcript. Measurements on this machine: Whisper 1.2 s/690 MiB, Kokoro
  8.2 s/1.35 GiB, ~10 s and 1.7 GiB peak. Application startup still does not
  load weights; with availability in `loading`, the audio buttons wait instead
  of receiving `BUSY`.
- **OpenCode credential validated before any request** (`bcdfc56`): for the
  official destination, the key must have the format `oc_sk_…`; values of
  another shape (for example `sk_<uuid>`) are refused when saving and detected
  locally, without `HTTP 401` and without cost. The 401 error now says that the
  provider refused the key and where to replace it; the refusal to advance
  indicates the «Seguimento IA» section. See the
  [recorded diagnostics](../opencode-go/validation.md).
- **Documentation of the article conversation mode** (`6c5f3fe`, `112e87d`):
  approved design and plan. It is planning; there is no implementation in this
  release.

Functional detail in [OpenCode Go](../opencode-go/validation.md),
[per-question evaluation](../ai-coaching/validation.md) and
[diagnostics](../diagnostics/validation.md).

## Limitations and pending items

- The **installer was not installed** on this machine: packaged installation,
  upgrade over v0.1.0 and uninstallation remain to be validated.
- Phases 1 to 4 still have no recorded manual acceptance; real audio,
  microphone, Kokoro voice and semantic usefulness pending.
- No paid request has been made. The acceptance of the tutor in the Go modality
  and the real cost remain to be confirmed; the stored key must be replaced with
  a valid `oc_sk_` one (see [diagnostics](../opencode-go/validation.md)).
- Local mode requires Python 3.11, the `audio-worker` helper and the Whisper
  `small`/Kokoro-82M models prepared outside the repository (see
  [phase 1 setup](../phase-1/setup.md)). The release does not include them.
- The textual evaluation is not a diagnosis of pronunciation, fluency or
  confidence.
